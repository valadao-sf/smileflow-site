# SmileFlow Site — real leads

Inherits core-agents. Local facts only.

Owns: smileflow.com.br and its acquisition interactions. Product behavior lives in smileflow.
Win: a real lead submitted.

Loop: approved offer + the visitor's next action → reuse current pages and components → publish
to the live site → submit the real form or CTA at the exact URL → fix forward. Static copy edits
need no full app build.

How things work: Nath intake stores contact, voice and attachments. Privileged keys run
server-side; uploads are signed and scoped by UUID in the controlled bucket/path; the captured
form version is tied to consent.
Proof: a working submission and its receipt.
